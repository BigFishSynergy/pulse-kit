## 5. Close rate and demo conversion

These two sound alike and answer different questions, so we keep both and name them carefully.

**Close rate** (the scoreboard number): approvals in the period divided by demos in the period. A roof signed September 2 from an August demo counts in September. It's final the day the period ends. Use it to grade reps and the team. By default it covers today's sales roster only.

**Demo conversion**: demos in the period that ever signed, divided by demos in the period. Every sale is credited back to the demo that produced it, so it keeps rising for a while. Use it to judge lead sources, campaigns, towns and setters. (In AccuLynx this is the "Close % Overall Certified" report.)

**A demo** for both: a quoted appointment, minus call backs, property management, and the work types and dead reasons you choose (by default insurance, inspection, retail, service and warranty work are left out).

Your answers: keep both? Which work types are left out of demos? Roster only, or the whole company, on the scoreboard?

## 6. Gross profit

One GP rule, used everywhere: jobs that went Closed in the period, upgrades included, call backs out. GP % is total profit divided by total contract amount. Profit is only real once a job is closed, so completed and invoiced jobs never count, even when they show a number. Closed jobs with $0 profit or 100% profit (costs not loaded) are left out and flagged.
