# Speed Round material (sample)

The weekly Speed Round asks about what is under each heading below. One "## Topic (number of questions)" heading per topic; the counts add up to at least 10. Write it in plain words: Claude only uses what is written here, and never invents a policy, price or warranty. This sample is for a made-up company; a real client replaces it with their own core values, sales process and products.

## Core values (4)

Summit Sample Roofing's core values:
1. Show up on time, every time. We call ahead if we are even five minutes late.
2. Tell the truth about the roof. If it only needs a repair, we say so.
3. Leave it cleaner than we found it. Magnet sweep for nails every day of the job.
4. Own the follow-up. Every homeowner question gets an answer the same business day.
5. Win together. Sales, production and the office share one scoreboard.

Our promise to homeowners: a written quote at the kitchen table, no deposit until materials are ordered, and a 10-year workmanship warranty on every full replacement.

## Sales process (3)

Our sales process, in order: 1) Confirm the appointment the day before. 2) Inspect the roof and take photos before talking price. 3) Walk the homeowner through the photos. 4) Present three options (repair, good, best). 5) Ask for the decision at the table. 6) If they need time, set the follow-up date before you leave.
The three most common objections and our answer: price (show the options and financing), "I need to get other quotes" (offer to leave a written quote and set a follow-up), and "I need to talk to my spouse" (offer a call or a second visit with both).

## Roofing basics (3)

Use widely documented roofing basics: shingle types (3-tab, architectural, designer), what underlayment and ice and water shield do, why attic ventilation matters, signs of hail and wind damage, and how flashing protects valleys, chimneys and walls. No exact warranty years or prices.
