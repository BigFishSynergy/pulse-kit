# Avatars

The 25 avatars in this folder are from **Notionists** by Zoish (https://heyzoish.gumroad.com/l/notionists), made with DiceBear (https://www.dicebear.com). Notionists is released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain): free to use, change and share for any purpose, including commercial, with no credit required.

Remade with `node scripts/make_avatars.mjs site/public/pulse/avatars` (after `npm install @dicebear/core@9.2.2 @dicebear/collection@9.2.2` in a scratch folder) from the seeds in index.json.
